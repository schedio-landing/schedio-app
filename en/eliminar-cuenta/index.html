Delete your Schedio account

This is an informational translation provided for convenience. The Spanish version is the legally binding one — if anything here conflicts with it, the Spanish text applies.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Below we explain how to do it, what gets deleted, what we keep, and for how long.

01 How to delete your account

From the app

Settings → Delete account → confirm the action.

By email

Send an email to schedio.contacto@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account", stating the email associated with your account. You don't need the app installed to do this.

If you signed up with Google, email is the only option available today: in-app deletion doesn't support that case yet.

02 What data gets deleted

  • Profile data (name, email, photo)
  • Study session history
  • Subjects, exams, and tasks on record
  • Files and notes uploaded to the Mochila
  • Progress, streaks, and statistics

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

03 What data we keep

  • Billing and subscription data — kept for 5 years due to legal obligation (tax and accounting regulations).

04 Deletion timeframe

Your account and associated data are deleted within 30 days at most from your request.

Questions about this process? Contact us at schedio.contacto@gmail.com. You can also check the privacy policy.
