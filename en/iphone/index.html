Install Schedio
without the App Store

Schedio isn't on the App Store yet. In the meantime, it installs as a web app: the icon ends up on your home screen just like any other app, it opens full-screen, and it doesn't carry Safari's toolbar over it. Four steps, two minutes.

It only works from Safari — that's an iOS limitation, not ours: if you open schedio.es from Chrome or from Instagram, you won't find the option to add it to your home screen.

  1. STEP 1

    Open schedio.es in Safari

    If you're already reading this from your iPhone in Safari, perfect — you can follow along as you read. If not, open Safari and type schedio.es in the address bar.

    schedio.es
  2. STEP 2

    Tap the Share icon

    It's the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar (on iPad it's at the top right). A panel opens up from the bottom of the screen.

    schedio.es
  3. STEP 3

    Find "Add to Home Screen"

    The row of icons up top is for sharing to other apps — that's not it. Scroll down the list of actions until you find "Add to Home Screen", with an icon of a square with a + sign.

    If you don't see it right away, scroll the list further down
    Add to Favorites
    Add to Quick Note
    Add to Home Screen
    Find on Page
  4. STEP 4

    Confirm with "Add"

    iOS shows you what the icon and its name will look like — you can leave it as is, it says "Schedio". Tap "Add" in the top right, and that's it: the icon shows up on your home screen, ready to open like any other app.

And that's it — here's how it looks

Schedio's icon on your home screen, just like any other app. Tapping it opens full-screen, no Safari toolbar.

Before you ask why notifications aren't coming through

On iPhone, notifications for an app installed this way only work if you have iOS 16.4 or later, and only after opening it at least once from the icon (not from Safari) and accepting the permission when it's asked for. That's an iOS limitation for web apps, not something that depends on Schedio.

This is a workaround until the native version arrives. As soon as Schedio is on the App Store, we'll update this page — and if you're on the waitlist, we'll email you. Something not working? Write to us at schedio.contacto@gmail.com.
