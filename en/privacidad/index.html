Schedio's Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This is an informational translation provided for convenience. The Spanish version is the legally binding one — if anything here conflicts with it, the Spanish text applies.

This policy explains what personal data Schedio collects, what we use it for, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPD-GDD).

01 Data controller

Service

Schedio

Contact

schedio.contacto@gmail.com

You can use that email for any question about this policy or to exercise the rights described below, including requesting deletion of your account and your data.

02 Who Schedio is for

Schedio is built for high school and university students, roughly between 16 and 22 years old. Spanish law (Article 7 of the LOPD-GDD) allows anyone over 14 to give their own consent for the processing of their personal data, so we don't ask for parental or guardian consent to use the app. If you're under 14, you shouldn't sign up for Schedio.

04 Legal basis for processing

Performance of a contract

Account, calendar, Mochila, and progress data are necessary to provide the service you request when you sign up.

Consent

For AI-generated recommendations and for push notifications (explicitly requested when you accept the permission). Also for the email and answers from the waitlist form: there's no usage contract yet at that point, so the only basis is the consent checkbox you tick when submitting it, which you can withdraw anytime.

Legitimate interest

Minimum security measures and abuse prevention (for example, limiting login attempts), and diagnosing technical failures (Crashlytics).

05 Who we share your data with

Schedio doesn't sell your data to anyone. The following providers process data on our behalf, as processors, solely to provide the service:

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Cloud Functions)

Hosts your account, your calendar, your progress, and your Mochila files, sends push notifications, and collects technical crash data. Firestore/Storage data is stored in the European region (eur3).

Google Gemini API

When you use the AI recommendations, your name and academic profile (subjects, grades, upcoming exams, study patterns) are sent to this Google API to generate the recommendation text. Your email, password, and Mochila files are not sent.

RevenueCat

Manages the status of your Schedio Prime subscription based on your purchase on Google Play/App Store; it doesn't see your email or your academic data.

Google Play / App Store

Process the Schedio Prime payment directly; Schedio doesn't see or store your payment data.

Since Google (Firebase, Gemini) and RevenueCat are companies based outside the European Union, these transfers are carried out under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or other equivalent mechanisms each provider has published).

06 How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, subjects, exams, sessions, streaks, and Mochila files are deleted. A minimal set of records (for example, those needed to justify a purchase already made) may be kept for as long as required by applicable tax or commercial regulations.

If you joined the waitlist from the website without creating an account, we keep your email and your answers until we launch Schedio and notify you, or until you ask to be removed before that by writing to schedio.contacto@gmail.com.

07 How to delete your account and your data

You can do this two ways, without needing the app installed:

From the app

Settings → Delete account, if you signed up with email and password. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

By email, without opening the app

Write to schedio.contacto@gmail.com requesting the deletion of your account and your data, from the same email you signed up with. We handle it within 30 days at most and confirm once it's done. This is also the only option currently available for accounts registered with Google, since in-app deletion doesn't support that case yet.

In both cases, this permanently deletes: your profile, subjects, exams, study sessions, streaks, Mochila files, and your access account. See the full process.

08 Your rights

Besides deletion (previous point), you can exercise the following at any time by writing to schedio.contacto@gmail.com:

Access

Know what data we have about you.

Rectification

Correct inaccurate data.

Portability

Receive your data in a reusable format.

Objection and restriction

Object to specific processing or ask us to pause it.

If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

09 Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is further protected by Firestore and Storage security rules, which restrict every document and file to its own owner. Passwords are never stored in plain text: they're managed by Firebase Authentication.

10 Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to what data we collect or who we share it with, we'll reflect it here by updating the date at the top and, if the change is substantial, we'll notify you inside the app.

Questions about this policy? Write to us at schedio.contacto@gmail.com
