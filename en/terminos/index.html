01 What Schedio is

Schedio is an academic organization app for high school and university students. It organizes your exam calendar, your subjects, and your study sessions, and gives you recommendations on how to divide your time.

Schedio doesn't teach educational content and doesn't replace your teachers, books, or study materials: it only helps you organize them.
