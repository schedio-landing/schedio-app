Why it exists

Make studying
easy

A company didn't make this. I'm making it, and I was just where you are.

I've been exactly where you are: stressed the week before an exam, not knowing where to start. Schedio isn't trying to teach you anything, because what I was missing wasn't notes — it was knowing what came next today.

That's why the app has no lessons, no videos, no chat that solves the exercise for you. It just clears out the one thing that stops you before you start: deciding.

And it's still being built with students. What the people using it say changes the app: the last three things added were requested by them.

FAQ

Common questions

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. On iPhone, Schedio works as a web app: you add it to your home screen and it behaves just like an app, no App Store needed. A native version is coming later. The first native release is for Android. See how to install it.

I already use my phone's calendar. What does this give me that it doesn't?

A calendar stores what you put into it and hands it back unchanged: it knows you have an exam on Thursday, but it has no idea what to do with that afternoon. Schedio decides what order to study in, how much time to give each thing, and what's next today, by weighing how close each exam is against your grade and how hard that subject is for you. That's the difference between writing something down and actually being organized.

I'm in university. Does it still work for me?

Yes. Swap terms for semesters and exams for midterms and finals, and it works the same: Schedio cares about dates, hours, and how much is riding on each subject. On top of that, the university profile keeps your academic record: degree credits, average grade, and what you could reach. It's usually even more useful in university, because nobody's setting the pace for you anymore.

Does it work for high school? What about university entrance exams?

Yes to both. Schedio doesn't depend on any specific curriculum: it organizes subjects, exams, and hours, and that works the same whether you're a freshman or cramming for a university entrance exam. For the final year it includes an entrance-grade calculator, with a countdown to the exams.

Does Schedio teach me the material?

No, on purpose. Schedio doesn't teach: it clears out the work of deciding what to study and when, which is where most of the energy goes before you even start.

What do you do with my data?

We store your subjects, exams, and sessions so the app works. We don't sell or share it with third parties. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.
